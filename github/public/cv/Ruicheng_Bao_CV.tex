\section{Research Profile}

Theoretical statistical physicist seeking general principles governing complex nonequilibrium systems, with a particular focus on randomness and timescales. My work spans stochastic thermodynamics, first-passage phenomena, relaxation and response, thermodynamic inference and optimal control, active matter, and open quantum dynamics.
